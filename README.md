### About

I'm a Robotics & AI Engineer joining **[Motion1](https://motion1.ai)** (June 2026), a stealth-stage startup building an AI copilot for humanoid robot workflows. I'm finishing my **MS in AI (Robotics)** at Northeastern University (3.95 GPA) and have spent the last two years building end-to-end systems across manipulation, autonomous navigation, teleoperation, and perception — from simulation to real hardware.

My work lives at the intersection of **physical AI, computer vision, and embodied control**. I care about making robots actually work in the real world.

Previously: surgical robotics at Articulus Surgical, SLAM-based autonomous docking at ARTPARK/IISc Bangalore, and leading the autonomous navigation team at Mars Rover Manipal (international competition placements).

|---|---|---|

| **Autonomous Navigation Stack** | ROS2 · Gazebo · IMU · LiDAR | Three-phase stack: IMU/LiDAR sensor fusion → SLAM-based localization → A\* global + DWA local planning. |

| **MuJoCo Humanoid Pick-and-Place** | MuJoCo · Python · 3D scene import | Extended Motion1's simulation pipeline to support full 3D scene import for humanoid pick-and-place demos. |
